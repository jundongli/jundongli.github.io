Biography

Jundong Li is an Associate Professor and Copenhaver Fellow at the University of Virginia with appointments in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering and the Department of Computer Science. Prior to joining UVA, he received his Ph.D. degree in Computer Science at Arizona State University in 2019 under the supervision of Dr. Huan Liu, M.Sc. degree in Computer Science at University of Alberta in 2014, and B.Eng. degree in Software Engineering at Zhejiang University in 2012.

His research interests span data mining, machine learning, and artificial intelligence, with a particular emphasis on LLM reasoning and post-training, knowledge and memory in LLMs, interpretability and trustworthy AI, graph machine learning, and AI for science and health. He has published more than 250 papers in high-impact venues, and his work has received over 25,000 citations. He has been recognized with several notable honors, including four early career awards—ICDM Tao Li Award (2025), SIGKDD Rising Star Award (2024), PAKDD Early Career Research Award (2023), and the NSF CAREER Award (2022). He has also received two best paper awards, namely the PAKDD Best Paper Award (2024) and the SIGKDD Best Research Paper Award (2022), as well as multiple industry faculty research awards.

His group's research is generously supported by UVA, NSF (CAREER, III, SaTC, SAI, S&CC, IIBR), NIH (RO1), DOE, ONR, Commonwealth Cyber Initiative, Jefferson Lab, Meta, Google, Netflix, Snap, JP Morgan, Cisco, and NEC Labs America.

Research

LLMs and graph learning models are increasingly deployed in high-stakes settings, yet they still reason wastefully, struggle to update and use knowledge reliably, and behave in ways that are hard to explain or verify. Our group works across four connected axes: reasoning and post-training, knowledge and memory, interpretability and trustworthiness, and graph machine learning. We apply these methods to scientific discovery, healthcare, and personalized recommendation.

  • LLM Reasoning and Post-Training — Token-efficient reasoning (SemCoT, NeurIPS'25; IAPO, ICML'26), reasoning distillation (QR-Distill, EMNLP'25; TeacherGRPO, EMNLP Findings'26), multimodal agent reasoning (SAP, NeurIPS'26), multi-agent collaboration (AnyMAC, EMNLP'25), preference optimization for recommendation (Rank-GRPO, ICLR'26; Mult-DPO, NeurIPS'26).
  • Knowledge, Memory, and Retrieval — Conflict-aware knowledge updating (LOKA, ACL'26), evolving agent memory (Janus, EMNLP'26), Graph-RAG over knowledge graphs (GGR, COLM'25; LSP-RAG, ICDM'26), robust retrieval-augmented generation (WinnowRAG, EMNLP'25), demonstration selection for in-context learning (MAPLE, ICML'25; SMILE, ICML'26).
  • Interpretability and Trustworthy AI — Mechanistic interpretability (ModCirc, ICML'25; REdit, ICLR'26), safety alignment under fine-tuning (NEST, NeurIPS'26), causal inference and causal reasoning (CiVAE, ICLR'25; CausalFlip, KDD'26), fairness evaluation and certification (CEB, ICLR'25 Spotlight; ELEGANT, KDD'26).
  • Graph Machine Learning — Graph prompting (Edge Prompt Tuning, ICLR'25; GraphTOP, NeurIPS'25), spectral analysis of GNNs (Spectral Benchmark, ICLR'25), federated graph learning (FedVN, AAAI'25; FedTAG, LoG'26), spatial-temporal forecasting (ST-FiT, AAAI'25; AdaST, NeurIPS'26).
  • AI for Science and Health — Protein and molecule modeling (EBM-DDG, KDD'25; MolEdit, WSDM'26), single-cell analysis (CeLLTra, Bioinformatics'25), brain network analysis (BrainMAP, AAAI'25; BrainTAP, ISBI'26), clinical risk prediction (UHES, ICHI'26), particle accelerator operations (BeamExplainer, TMLR'26).

LLM Reasoning and Post-Training

Reasoning models are powerful but costly. We make LLM reasoning more efficient and better targeted through token-efficient and implicit chain-of-thought, reasoning distillation, multi-route and multi-agent inference, model fusion, and post-training for LLM-based recommendation.

  • EMNLP Findings'26 TeacherGRPO: Closing the Capacity Gap in Reasoning Distillation via Teacher Alignment
  • NeurIPS'26 Saliency-Aware Multi-Route Thinking: Grounding and Reasoning on Vision-Language Agents
  • ICLR'26 Rank-GRPO: Training LLM-based Conversational Recommender Systems with Reinforcement Learning
  • NeurIPS'26 Mult-DPO: Multinomial Direct Preference Optimization for Recommender Systems
  • NeurIPS'25 SemCoT: Accelerating Chain-of-Thought Reasoning through Semantically-Aligned Implicit Tokens
  • EMNLP'25 Learning from Diverse Reasoning Paths with Routing and Collaboration
